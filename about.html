Investment Model

An angel syndicate built on trust, transparency, and a shared alma mater.

01

Deal-by-Deal

All investments are opt-in. No commitment beyond what excites you. Minimum $5K per deal.

02

Seed Stage Focus

$100K–$150K checks into seed rounds of $500K–$1M alongside a lead investor, via SAFEs, convertible notes, or preferred stock.

03

No Membership Fees

No fees from 99TAA. AngelList charges standard SPV fees at the time of investment. 20% carry on returns, split between the lead and the org.

04

Mentorship Network

Every portfolio company gets a dedicated mentor from our 150+ alumni network.

05

Nationwide Reach

We invest in any U.S.-based company with CMU ties — not limited to Pittsburgh.
