99TAA does not charge membership or management fees. AngelList charges standard SPV setup and administration fees to investors at the time they wire money.

Investment instruments used: SAFEs, convertible notes, AND preferred stock (not just SAFEs).

Tax filings: AngelList manages tax filings and K-1 distribution through Belltower — 99TAA does not handle this directly.

**Why:** Feedback from stakeholder on 2026-04-03 to ensure site accurately represents the syndicate's operations.
**How to apply:** When writing or editing site copy about fees, deal structure, or post-investment admin, use these facts as the source of truth.
